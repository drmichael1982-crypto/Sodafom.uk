How to look around

Swipe the picture, use the arrow buttons, or focus the picture and use your keyboard arrows. To look by turning and tilting your phone, tap “Move phone to look” and allow movement access if asked. You can stay seated. Tap “Use swipe controls” to stop phone movement.

This preview places the Sun ahead, with Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune arranged around you. Look up for stars and down for the observation deck. The layout and sizes are for exploring the controls; they are not a scale model of the solar system.
